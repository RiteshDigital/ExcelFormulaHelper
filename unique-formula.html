UNIQUE Formula in Excel

Extract unique values from a list or range.

What is UNIQUE?

UNIQUE creates a dynamic list containing distinct values from a range.

Example

=UNIQUE(A2:A100)

The result spills automatically into the cells below.

Sort unique values

=SORT(UNIQUE(A2:A100))
Tip: UNIQUE is useful for creating dynamic lists for reports and dropdown preparation.
