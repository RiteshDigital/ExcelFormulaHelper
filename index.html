Useful Excel Formula Tips

These simple practices can make Excel formulas easier to build, understand and maintain.

  • Use absolute references such as $A$1 when a cell reference should not change.
  • Use IFERROR when you need to display a useful result instead of a formula error.
  • Use XLOOKUP for flexible lookup tasks in supported Excel versions.
  • Use SUMIFS and COUNTIFS when your calculation depends on multiple conditions.
  • Use FILTER and UNIQUE to work with dynamic lists in supported Excel versions.
  • Check your cell ranges carefully when working with large datasets.
  • Test formulas with a small sample of data before applying them to a large workbook.
