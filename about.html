About Excel Formula Helper

Excel Formula Helper is a free online resource designed to help people find practical Microsoft Excel formulas using simple descriptions of everyday spreadsheet problems.

What we provide

  • Formula examples for common Excel tasks.
  • Lookup, calculation, logic, text, date and data-management formulas.
  • Alternative formulas where useful.
  • Simple explanations so users can understand how a formula works.

Our goal

Our goal is to make Excel easier for beginners, students, analysts, office professionals and anyone who works with spreadsheets.

Important

The formulas on this website are examples. Excel versions, workbook structures and regional settings can affect results. Always test a formula with your own data before using it in important work.
